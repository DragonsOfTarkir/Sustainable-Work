Work that sustains the people who do it.

The Sustainable Work Lab at Penn State Abington studies job quality, work design and working time, and shares its research with students, scholars and the public.

Browse the research Contact the director

What the lab studies

Three lines of research, each looking at how work can be better for the people doing it.

  • Job quality

    How pay, hours, autonomy and security shape what a job is like and what it does to the people in it.

    Browse job quality research

  • Salutogenic work design

    Designing work around what keeps people healthy, rather than only reducing what harms them.

    Browse work design research

  • Golden hours and SHOWWW

    The lab's work on working time, and on schedules that leave room for the rest of life.

    Browse working time research
